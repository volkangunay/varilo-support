Help for Budget Planner & Bill Tracker.

Start a book

Create a book, choose its currency, and enter your opening balance and optional reserve. Use + to record actual income or expenses, or add a planned expense or expected income. A book's currency is fixed after creation to prevent accidental conversion.

Understand your balance

Current balance includes the opening balance and recorded transactions only. After bills and reserve subtracts unpaid expenses due within 30 days, overdue expenses and your reserve. Expected income is excluded from this figure. The Plan tab shows an estimate that can include expected income. These are manual projections based on your entries, not bank balances or guarantees.

Record payments

In Plan, tap an upcoming item and record it as paid or received when it actually happens. The same occurrence cannot be recorded twice. Editing a recurring plan changes its future schedule; stopping a plan preserves already recorded transactions.

Free and Pro

One book, unlimited entries, 30-day planning, CSV export and backup are free. Pro adds multiple books, a 90-day forecast and income-delay scenarios. Prices and periods are shown in your local App Store currency before purchase. Manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account subscription settings. Use Restore purchases in the Pro screen on the same Apple Account.

Protect your records

Use Books → Save backup to keep a full copy. CSV exports contain transactions; the JSON backup also contains plans and settings. Save a backup before switching devices. Restoring replaces all books. If storage cannot be read, the app preserves existing files instead of starting over.
