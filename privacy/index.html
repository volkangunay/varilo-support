Your money records stay with you.

Privacy policy · Effective October 3, 2026

Varilo, listed as Budget Planner & Bill Tracker, is published by Vomcore Teknoloji Limited Sirketi. It is a manual personal budgeting app.

Records on your device

Your book names, currency, opening balance, reserve, income, expenses and planned payments are stored in the app's local storage. The app does not upload these records to us, connect to your bank, require an account, or include advertising or analytics SDKs. The app keeps the previous saved file locally to protect against a failed save. Device backups managed by Apple may include app data, depending on your device settings.

Purchases

Optional subscriptions use Apple's StoreKit. Apple processes payment and supplies the app with verified purchase information so it can unlock Pro. We do not receive your payment card details or link purchases to your financial records. Apple's privacy terms apply to its payment and account services.

Export and restore

You can export transaction CSV files or a full JSON backup using the system share sheet. These files contain the records you entered. The destination you choose receives the exported file and its own privacy practices apply. Restoring a backup replaces your current books after confirmation. Export first if you want to retain them.

Deletion

You can delete individual records or a whole book. The previous local save can retain a deleted record until the next successful save. Deleting the app removes its local storage. Exports and existing device backups are not removed by deleting records or the app.

Contact

For support or privacy requests, email hello@vomcore.com. If you email us, we use the information you provide to answer your request. Please avoid sending personal financial records unless necessary.

Changes

We will update this policy and its effective date if our data practices change.
